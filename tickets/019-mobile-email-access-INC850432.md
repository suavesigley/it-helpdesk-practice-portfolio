# INC850432 – Cannot Access Email on Mobile Device

**Priority:** Medium  
**Request Type:** Mobile Email / Client Troubleshooting  
**User:** Ivana Petrova (`ipetrova`)  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Location:** Remote (Working from home)  
**Date Completed:** September 17, 2026

## Summary
Remote user reported that work email was no longer arriving on her phone, showing the error “Cannot connect to server.” Email continued to work normally on her laptop. Phone internet and other apps were functioning. This prevented her from checking urgent messages while away from her desk.

## Steps Taken
1. Claimed the ticket.
2. Confirmed with the user that email was still accessible on her laptop (ruling out an account-level issue).
3. Determined the mobile email app was likely in a bad state.
4. Instructed the user to uninstall the email app from her phone.
5. Had the user reinstall the app and sign back in with her work credentials.
6. Confirmed that emails began syncing successfully on the mobile device.
7. Added resolution notes and closed the ticket.

## Resolution
The mobile email application was in a corrupted or stale state.  
Uninstalling, reinstalling, and signing back in restored full email access on the phone.
