# INC178363 – Shared Warehouse Laptop Completely Dead

**Priority:** Critical  
**Request Type:** Hardware Replacement (Shared Laptop)  
**Reported by:** Carlos Rodriguez (Facilities)  
**Location:** Warehouse / Floor 1  
**Date Completed:** November 3, 2026

## Summary
The shared laptop used on the warehouse inventory cart would not power on at all (no lights, no fan). Multiple chargers and a known-good outlet were tested. This blocked the entire warehouse team from performing stock scanning and inventory logging on a day when counts were due.

## Steps Taken
1. Claimed the Critical ticket.
2. Confirmed the device was a laptop and that it had a complete power failure.
3. Built a replacement laptop in Computer Deployment using **Manual Domain Enrollment** (correct method for shared Facilities / warehouse equipment).
4. Completed the domain join and initial setup.
5. Registered the new laptop as an asset and set status to “Ready to deploy”.
6. Checked out the replacement via Asset Management (Site view).
7. Obtained the site receiving dock address and shipped the laptop to:  
   1200 Commerce Park Drive, Columbus, OH 43215
8. Included a return label for the failed unit.
9. Confirmed the replacement was on its way so inventory work could resume.
10. Added resolution notes and closed the ticket.

## Resolution
The original shared laptop suffered a complete power failure.  
A properly configured replacement laptop was built via Manual Domain Enrollment, registered, checked out, and shipped to the site receiving dock with a return label.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Critical shared-device hardware replacement
- Correct selection of Manual Domain Enrollment for Facilities/warehouse equipment
- Domain join and post-build configuration
- Asset registration and checkout process
- Shipping shared equipment to the correct site receiving dock
- End-to-end replacement under operational time pressure
