# INC785823 – People on Company Chat Calls Cannot Hear Me

**Priority:** Medium  
**Request Type:** Audio / Hardware Driver  
**User:** Ivana Petrova (`ipetrova`)  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Location:** Remote (Working from home)  
**Date Completed:** October 17, 2026

## Summary
Remote user reported that colleagues could not hear her on Company Chat calls. The microphone was not picking up any audio in any application (Company Chat, Video Meet, or the Windows microphone test). She could hear others normally. This prevented her from participating in team meetings and sprint planning.

## Steps Taken
1. Claimed the ticket and initiated a remote support session.
2. Checked for audio driver updates through normal system channels – none were available.
3. Identified the correct hardware model (Audiomax AM-70M).
4. Manually downloaded the official Audiomax AM-70M driver via web browser.
5. Installed and configured the driver.
6. Tested the microphone and confirmed it was now working in applications.
7. Added resolution notes and closed the ticket.

## Resolution
The microphone failure was caused by a missing or corrupted audio driver.  
After manually downloading and installing the correct Audiomax AM-70M driver, full microphone functionality was restored.
