# INC463220 – No Email Arriving After Return From Leave

**Priority:** High  
**Request Type:** Account / Mailbox Access  
**User:** Priya Sharma (`psharma`)  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Location:** Floor 1  
**Date Completed:** October 26, 2026

## Summary
User returned from approximately six weeks of extended leave. She could sign in successfully and had access to her calendar and files, but no email was arriving. Colleagues reported that messages sent to her were bouncing. The inbox was also empty in webmail, confirming this was not a client-side issue.

## Steps Taken
1. Claimed the high-priority ticket.
2. Reviewed the user’s account in Directory.
3. Identified that the email / Exchange licence had been removed during the period of leave.
4. Re-assigned the appropriate email licence to the account.
5. Verified that new test messages were now being delivered successfully to both the desktop client and webmail.
6. Added resolution notes and closed the ticket.

## Resolution
The mailbox was unable to receive email because the associated licence had been removed while the user was on extended leave.  
Re-assigning the licence immediately restored full email delivery.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Diagnosing account-level email delivery failures
- Checking and restoring user licences after extended absence
- Differentiating client-side vs mailbox/licence issues
- Restoring critical communication access for a returning employee
