# INC120296 – Second Monitor Black Screen / Not Detected

**Priority:** Medium  
**Request Type:** Hardware / Display Troubleshooting  
**User:** Kevin Zhang (`kzhang`)  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Location:** Floor 3  
**Date Completed:** September 2, 2026

## Summary
User reported that their second monitor was no longer detected after a system update the previous night. Display settings only showed one monitor. The monitor itself was powered on and the cable was connected. The issue significantly reduced productivity as the user relies on a dual-monitor setup.

## Steps Taken
1. Claimed the ticket and initiated a remote support session to the user’s workstation.
2. Observed slight display glitching on the remote session.
3. Opened Settings and checked for available updates — multiple updates were pending.
4. Installed all available system, driver, and firmware updates.
5. Restarted the workstation.
6. Reconnected after the restart and confirmed both monitors were detected and functioning correctly.
7. Added resolution notes and closed the ticket.

## Resolution
The second monitor issue was caused by outdated or incomplete drivers following a system update.  
Installing all pending updates and restarting the PC restored full dual-monitor functionality.
